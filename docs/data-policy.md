# Data policy

- No real patient, client, hospital, or business records are permitted.
- Policy sources must have a recorded URL, retrieval date, version, checksum, and license/use note.
- Ambiguous-license source files stay outside Git; manifests and reproducible download instructions may be retained.
- Synthetic claims are project-owned fixtures and must be labeled synthetic in every interface and report.
- Logs store hashes and redacted summaries rather than secrets or unnecessary record contents.
- Source-backed answers must distinguish Indonesian policy from any optional non-Indonesian reference material.
